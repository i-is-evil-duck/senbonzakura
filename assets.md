## Notes
- Textures should be 16x16 or 32x32 PNG files
- Item icons are rendered automatically from textures in creative inventory
- Textures go in: `src/main/resources/assets/senbonzakura/textures/item/`
- Without textures, items will appear as pink/black squares in-game
