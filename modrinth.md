# Senbonzakura

This mod adds 8 new weapons, each with unique charge-activated abilities, passive hit-count bonuses, and effects.
## Features

### Unique Weapons

- **Senbonzakura** - Charge and release to dash 12 blocks forward with a sweeping slash.
- **Crescent Blade of Winds** - 360° slash; dual-wield for 2x damage.
- **Crypt Blade** - Hold 15s to charge an explosion (6-block radius, no block grief). Immobile with Resistance II during charge.
- **Rite of Commandments** - Grants Absorption IV + Regeneration II for 8 seconds.
- **Pyrerizer** - Fires a fire projectile that deals 6 damage + burn effect.
- **Yuki no Ame** - Summons a snowstorm that applies Slowness III in an 8-block radius.
- **The Finale** - 3s charge for a massive 10-damage projectile with explosion.
- **Saw Blade Axe** - Mines wood at Diamond Axe speed.

### Hit-Count Passives

Land 4-5 consecutive hits to trigger bonus damage on any weapon.

### Charge & Cooldown System

Hold right-click to charge, release to activate. Each weapon has a cooldown ranging from 5s to 30s.

### Crafting Progression

16 intermediate items (Archaic Crown, Inky Pearl, Exalted Blossom, etc.) are obtained through mob drops, block interactions, and crafting - leading up to the final weapons via Smithing Table recipes.


## Compatibility

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1
